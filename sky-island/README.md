# The sky island

A side quest with a place of its own: an island floating high above the sea north of Outset, with ruins round a
plaza, waterfalls pouring off its edges and a boss arena behind a locked door. Needs the Story Notebook package
(the port's story system) and a port with mod stages, stage light and stage trees.

What the player gets:

1. **The letter:** an old sailor's letter waits at a Rito postbox: a great bird has been seen over Outset's pier.
2. **The pier:** walking along Outset's pier towards the lookout, the Helmaroc King swoops down, grabs Link and
   carries him up above the clouds. The island comes into view far ahead; the bird lets go and Link drops onto it.
   (An updraft off Outset is a second way up.)
3. **The island:** grass terraces, the game's own trees, ruins of grey stone, a round plaza with the Hyrule crest in
   its middle, and the boss key in a chest among the ruins. The island has the light of the sea round Outset and
   follows the game's clock.
4. **The arena:** across the bridge, the boss door opens with the boss key. A darknut guards a big chest with a
   heart container.
5. **The way home:** once the chest is open, the warp light takes Link back to Outset, and the quest ends with a
   reward of 100 rupees.

The stage is built on the player's machine from the player's own game files and this package's models; nothing of
the game is in the package. The code only logs the quest's step while testing.

The heart container raises Link's hearts for good in the player's own save, as the game's own do.
